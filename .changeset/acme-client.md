The ACME client (RFC 8555), still with no dependency and no required peer. `new AcmeClient({ directoryUrl, accountKey, fetch? })` fetches the directory once and keeps it; keeps each answer's `Replay-Nonce` for the next request and fetches one by a HEAD to `newNonce` only when none is left; retries a `badNonce` refusal with the nonce it carries, 3 times at most; and offers `newAccount({ contact, termsOfServiceAgreed, onlyReturnExisting })` (the account URL, kept as `kid`), `newOrder({ identifiers })`, `order(url)`, `authorization(url)`, `challenge(url)` (POSTs `{}`), `waitForAuthorization` and `waitForOrder` (polling as `Retry-After` says, clamped from `pollIntervalMs` to a minute, within a `timeoutMs` and an `AbortSignal`), `finalize(order, csr)` and `certificate(url)`, the PEM chain, each certificate in it parsed and issued by the next. Every fetch is a POST-as-GET. Every URL, given or answered, must be `https:` (`allowInsecure` takes `http:`, for a test CA only); a redirect is refused; answers are read up to 256 KiB, a certificate chain up to 1 MiB; every number from the CA is clamped; the account key appears in no message and no `inspect`. `obtainCertificate({ client, names, certificateKey, http01: { set, remove } })` runs the whole HTTP-01 flow: it refuses an order or an authorization for other names than asked, and a certificate whose leaf is not for `certificateKey` and exactly those names, or has expired; and it always removes the tokens it set, even on a failure, an abort or a timeout, each once its `set` settled, all within one grace, a cleanup failure kept as the thrown error's `cause`. `http01Responder()` serves key authorizations from memory as a `Bun.serve` fetch handler, answering 404 to anything else. `AcmeError` gains `problem`, `status` and `retryAfter`, and the codes `NO_ACCOUNT`, `SERVER_PROBLEM`, `RATE_LIMITED`, `BAD_RESPONSE`, `NETWORK_ERROR`, `TIMEOUT`, `ABORTED`, `AUTHORIZATION_FAILED` and `ORDER_FAILED`. `signJws` now also refuses a URL longer than 2048 characters, by the same rule the client checks URLs with. Key rollover, revocation and renewal information (ARI) come later.
