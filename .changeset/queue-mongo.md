'@bumail/queue': minor

Add `@bumail/queue/mongo`: `MongoQueueStore`, the `QueueStore` contract on MongoDB through the driver the application already has, so instances of a server on several machines share one queue with no dependency and no peer. `MongoQueueStore.open({ db, collectionPrefix? })` takes a `Db` of the `mongodb` driver, 6 or later, typed by the methods the store calls (`MongoQueueDb`), and never closes it. Every write that decides — claim, renew, complete, reschedule, cancel — is one `findOneAndUpdate` or `findOneAndDelete` on one item's document, filtered on its lease (and, for an outcome, on how many were recorded), so two instances never take the same item and a crashed instance's items are claimed again once their leases expire, in due order, then the oldest. Messages are kept byte for byte as BSON binary in chunks of 4 MiB, so one larger than a document's 16 MiB fits. `maxItems` holds with no transaction — an item added with it holds a place a unique index gives one item at a time — so a standalone server works as well as a replica set. Every collection is written with `w: 'majority', j: true` and read with `readConcern: 'majority'` from the primary, whatever the client was opened with; the guide spells out the collections and indexes, what a failover can cost, and the roles a worker needs.
