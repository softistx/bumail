A new package, published for the first time: the primitives of an ACME client (RFC 8555), with no dependency and no peer. `createCsr({ names, keyPair })` builds a PKCS #10 request (RFC 2986) for DNS names — the subject `CN=<first name>`, the public key as Web Crypto exports it, an `extensionRequest` holding a `subjectAltName` with every name as a `dNSName` — signed with `ecdsa-with-SHA256` (the P1363 signature turned into DER) or `sha256WithRSAEncryption`, and returns its DER, its PEM and the names; names are checked first (ASCII DNS host names, IDNs as A-labels, no wildcard, no IP address, at most 100). `signJws` signs an ACME request as a flattened JWS (RFC 7515) with the protected header `{ alg, nonce, url, jwk | kid }`, ES256 or RS256, and the empty payload of a POST-as-GET. `jwkThumbprint` (RFC 7638), `publicJwk`, `keyAuthorization` and `http01Path` answer HTTP-01. `generateKeyPair` makes a P-256 or RSA-2048 pair, `exportPrivateKeyPem` writes the private key as PKCS #8 PEM that Bun's TLS takes, and `importKeyPairPem` reads it back. Every refusal is an `AcmeError` with a code: `INVALID_NAME`, `INVALID_OPTION`, `INVALID_KEY` or `INVALID_TOKEN`.
